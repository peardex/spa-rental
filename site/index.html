The home

Built for slowing down, together.

A 1970s timber house reworked with wide windows, warm oak floors and a kitchen big enough for everyone to help. Every bedroom has a view, and nobody gets the sofa bed.

The great room

Vaulted ceilings, a wood stove, a record player with a crate of vinyl, and a 14-foot window facing Mount Hood.

Cook's kitchen

Six-burner range, espresso machine, and a long walnut table that seats ten.

Four bedrooms

Two kings, a queen and a bunk room with two full beds. Linen sheets and blackout blinds throughout.

Wraparound deck

Morning coffee in the sun, dinner off the grill, and a fire pit for when the evening cools down.

The spa

Your own bathhouse in the trees.

Down a short stone path from the deck, the spa is yours alone. No booking slots, no shared towels.

FAQ

Good to know.

Is the hot tub private?

Yes. The spa sits on the property and is used only by guests staying at Juniper House.

How do we get there in winter?

The road is plowed by the county and the driveway by us. We recommend all-wheel drive or chains from December to February.

Is there Wi-Fi and a place to work?

Fiber internet at 500 Mbps and a desk with a monitor in the loft. Mobile signal is patchy, which most guests consider a feature.

Can we host an event?

Small celebrations of up to 8 guests are welcome. We don't host parties or weddings, and quiet hours start at 10pm.

What's nearby?

Orchards and wineries within 10 minutes, Hood River town in 20, and Mount Hood Meadows ski area in 35.
